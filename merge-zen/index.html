Merge Zen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Merge Zen is a casual puzzle game developed by Hakan Sözer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we do not collect

Merge Zen does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal files. Your game progress, scores and preferences are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, and ad interaction data (e.g. views and taps). If you decline tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Your choices

  • You can decline tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or change it anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored game data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
